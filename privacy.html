Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Off Course: 3D Golf for iPhone and Android

Off Course is developed and published by Craneshire LLC, a New York limited liability company (“we”). This policy explains how the Off Course app and this support website handle information. Contact eric@craneshire.co with privacy questions or requests.

The short version

  • We collect no personal data.
  • No account, no advertising and no tracking.
  • No location access.
  • The courses are built into the app, and the app doesn’t contact any server of ours.
  • If you choose Live wind, the app asks the U.S. National Weather Service for the forecast at the course’s location. It never sends your location.
  • On Android, the Google Play billing code inside the app sends Google technical logs each time the app checks for or makes the purchase.

No account, advertising or tracking

You don’t need an account to play. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries, doesn’t request an advertising identifier, and doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We don’t sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, such as sound, background sound, wind, tees, the plays-like display and the pick up at double par option, are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone, along with a few display preferences. If you use Live wind, the latest forecast for each course you’ve played with it is stored there too. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app removes them. On iPhone, Apple may include them in your phone’s backups, depending on your settings. The Android app leaves them out of Google backups and transfers to a new phone.

Network use

Every course ships inside the app, so playing doesn’t need the internet. The one exception is Live wind, a wind setting added in version 1.1 that you choose yourself: the app then asks the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), a government service, for the forecast at the course’s location. The request contains the course’s map location, which is the same for every player, not yours, and names the app and this website, as the Weather Service asks. Like any website, the Weather Service sees your internet address when the app connects; its privacy policy covers that. The forecast is kept on your phone for up to 12 hours, so Live wind works offline for a while. None of it comes to us.

We don’t operate a server for Off Course, and the app doesn’t send your play, scores or settings anywhere. It also goes online for the All courses purchase: showing its price, buying it and restoring it, and on Android for the billing logs described below. On iPhone that goes through the App Store, which Apple runs. On Android it goes through the Google Play app on your phone, which Google runs.

On Android, the purchase is handled inside the app by Google’s Play Billing Library. Each time the app opens or comes back, it asks the library whether All courses is unlocked, and the library sends Google technical logs about these checks and any purchase: for example, whether a request succeeded and any connection problems, with details of the phone and app such as the phone model, Android version, language and region settings, time zone, mobile carrier code, network type and the app’s version. Google says it uses these logs to improve the library and help with errors. They go to Google, not to us, and Google’s privacy policy governs them.

Location

Off Course doesn’t request or use your location. Live wind uses each course’s own map location, never yours.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store purchases and Google handles Google Play purchases. We don’t receive your card or bank details.

  • iPhone: Apple may provide us with sales reports and, if you choose in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, crash and performance reports. Apple’s settings and privacy policy govern those reports.
  • Android: Google provides us with order records and sales reports for purchases, which we use to handle refunds and taxes. If you choose in your phone’s settings to share usage and diagnostics, Google may also provide crash and performance reports. Google’s settings and privacy policy govern those records and reports.

Support email

If you email eric@craneshire.co, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it to answer you and fix problems, keep it only as long as that needs, and delete it when you ask. Please don’t send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Your choices

You can change sound, wind, tees and display settings in the app at any time. Live wind is off unless you choose it; choose another wind setting to stop it. Deleting the app removes its settings from your phone; existing iPhone backups follow your Apple settings. For anything you have emailed us, ask us to delete it.

This website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms or browser storage. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to other sites, such as OpenStreetMap, open services with their own privacy practices.

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If a child has emailed us, contact us and we’ll delete the message.

Changes

If the way Off Course handles information changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.
