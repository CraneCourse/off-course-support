Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Off Course: 3D Golf for iPhone and Android

Off Course is developed and published by Craneshire LLC, a New York limited liability company (“we”). This policy explains how the Off Course app and this support website handle information. Contact eric@craneshire.co with privacy questions or requests.

The short version

  • Off Course collects no personal data.
  • No account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No location access.
  • The courses are built into the app, and the app doesn’t contact any server of ours.

No account, advertising or tracking

You don’t need an account to play. The app contains no advertising and no analytics code from other companies, doesn’t request an advertising identifier, and doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We don’t sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, such as sound, birdsong, wind, tees, the plays-like display and the pick up at double par option, are stored in the app’s private storage on your phone, along with a few display preferences. They aren’t sent to us. Deleting the app removes them. Apple or Google may include them in your phone’s backups, depending on your settings.

Network use

Every course ships inside the app, so playing doesn’t use the internet. We don’t operate a server for Off Course, and the app doesn’t send your play, scores or settings anywhere. Its only online requests are for the All courses purchase: showing its price, buying it and restoring it. On iPhone they go through the App Store, which Apple runs. On Android they go through the Google Play app on your phone, which Google runs.

Location

Off Course doesn’t request or use your location.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store purchases and Google handles Google Play purchases. We don’t receive your card or bank details.

  • iPhone: Apple may provide us with sales reports and, if you choose in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, crash and performance reports. Apple’s settings and privacy policy govern those reports.
  • Android: Google provides us with order records and sales reports for purchases, which we use to handle refunds and taxes. If you choose in your phone’s settings to share usage and diagnostics, Google may also provide crash and performance reports. Google’s settings and privacy policy govern those records and reports.

Support email

If you email eric@craneshire.co, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it to answer you and fix problems, keep it only as long as that needs, and delete it when you ask. Please don’t send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Your choices

You can change sound, wind, tees and display settings in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its settings from your phone; existing backups follow your Apple or Google settings. For anything you have emailed us, ask us to delete it.

This website

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms or browser storage. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to other sites, such as OpenStreetMap, open services with their own privacy practices.

Children

Off Course collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If a child has emailed us, contact us and we’ll delete the message.

Changes

If the way Off Course handles information changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.
